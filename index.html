— offline
This is a first draft wired directly to your Sales / HR / Finance tools. Figures marked AUTO are pulled live — please sanity-check them against the source tools before this goes to OxeFit. Figures marked MANUAL are entered by you each week (see the relevant tab). See Data Health for exactly what loaded.

a Sales achieved vs. committed AUTO — sales tracker

FY-to-date and this-month, pulled from the weekly commit sheet. Achieved = max closed-won per rep across that month's weeks (avoids double-counting a snapshot repeated in several weeks); committed = sum of everyone's Commit value for the month. See Data Health for a note on when this can lag the tracker's live HubSpot refresh.

b Orders by product line — XS1 vs XP1 AUTO — sales tracker

Unit counts reported on the weekly commit sheet (forecastXS1 / forecastXP1 fields per rep, per week). This reflects what reps report weekly, not a separate confirmed-order ledger — flag it if you want this tied to invoiced orders instead.

c Finance — cost vs. income (OxeFit)

Salaries pull from HR; showroom/warehouse rent and marketing budget pull from Finance's central cost pool. "Income to OxeFit" is entered manually until it's wired to the Finance payables ledger.

Manual inputs MANUAL

d Showroom & warehouse relocation progress MANUAL — you update this weekly

Tracks progress on moving to replacement premises (ties into the leasing brief / agent outreach work) and the cost reduction that comes with it.

e FY target progress AUTO — sales tracker goals

Sum of monthly rep goals (perfMeta.goals) for Jan–Dec of the fiscal year, vs. cumulative closedWon to date.

Cost reduction target MANUAL

f Marketing leads generated this month AUTO — sales tracker

From the Marketing Leads / follow-up pipeline, filtered to leads created this calendar month.

g Employee status, achievements & closures AUTO + MANUAL notes

Status/role from HR, deals closed this month derived from the sales tracker. Add a one-line achievement note per person — it's saved for next week too.
